# Public content governance

## Approved position

Agila is an independent architecture and transformation practice helping
organisations turn complex operations, fragmented data and AI ambition into
governed systems and executable change.

The category descriptor is **AI-central, architecture-led transformation**.
Architecture makes AI executable, and AI expands what architecture can deliver.

## Message hierarchy

1. AI and agentic transformation is the primary value focus.
2. Enterprise, solution and integration architecture is the professional spine.
3. Industrial IT/OT and operational data is the differentiating field anchor.
4. Transformation design, governance, digital products, analytics, training and
   adoption support the three pillars; they are not equal catalogue businesses.
5. Delivery is principal-led, partner-enabled and agent-augmented, with human
   accountability retained throughout.

## Evidence-safe claims

The site may describe:

- more than eight years across manufacturing and enterprise transformation;
- enterprise and solution architecture, integration, event-driven systems and
  architecture governance;
- industrial IT/OT, digital manufacturing, MES, MQTT, Unified Namespace and
  operational-data architecture;
- AI and data readiness, governed agentic workflows and internal AI operating
  systems under human control;
- Luxembourg as the base and international applicability;
- anonymised work across manufacturing, aviation, specialist SMEs and internal
  AI operating systems;
- Agila's principal-led, partner-enabled and agent-augmented capacity model.

## Claims requiring a fresh approval record

Do not publish or expand any of the following without source, permission and
wording approval:

- client or target names, logos, testimonials, metrics and outcomes;
- detailed customer architectures, implementation status or production impact;
- current partner availability, legal partnerships, joint-delivery rights,
  employment or team-size implications;
- agent counts, current topology, runtime-health or autonomous-operation claims;
- legal, accounting, tax, cybersecurity-audit or regulated-AI sign-off authority;
- prices, packages, delivery duration, savings, ROI or guaranteed benefits;
- new accreditation, funding, subsidy, eligibility or programme-status claims.

## Content-change receipt

A content pull request should state:

- what changed and why;
- the approved source or owner decision supporting the change;
- whether a client, partner, programme, legal or outcome claim is affected;
- which checks passed;
- whether metadata, sitemap, privacy text or the social card must also change.
